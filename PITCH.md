# ScottyMeet

**The Tinder for Carnegie Mellon undergrads.**

ScottyMeet solves the awkwardness of dating on a tight-knit campus by providing a verified, CMU-only matching platform where students swipe through profiles filtered by college (SCS, CIT, CFA, Tepper, and more), graduation year, and shared interests. What makes it unique is the dual-mode system — toggle between **Dating** and **Study Date** to find romance or study partners — plus **Tartan Crush**, an anonymous signal feature where mutual crushes trigger a reveal. The fully interactive demo features drag-to-swipe cards with gesture physics, real-time chat, animated match popups, onboarding flow, and a polished landing page — all built in Next.js with framer-motion and shadcn/ui, designed to feel like a native mobile app.
